\chapter*{Acknowledgement}

We begin by thanking Almighty Allah, without whose grace we would not
have had the strength or the patience to see this thesis through.

We are grateful to our supervisor, Md Zunaid Hossen, Lecturer in the
Department of Mechatronics Engineering, RUET. He met with us regularly,
read every draft closely, and asked hard questions when our reasoning
was thin. The thesis is in better shape because of that.

We also thank the external examiner for the time spent reading and
evaluating this work. A careful outside reading is not easy to give,
and we appreciate it.

The Head of the Department of Mechatronics Engineering supported this
project from the start, and we thank him for that support.

Several other faculty members and the office staff of the department
helped along the way, whether by answering a quick question, arranging
lab access, or sorting out paperwork on short notice. We thank all of
them. Finally, we thank our families, who bore with the long hours this
work demanded and supported us through all of them.

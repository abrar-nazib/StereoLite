\section{Research Ethics Compliance}
This work involved no human subjects, no personal or sensitive data, and
no animal experimentation. The datasets used are public research
benchmarks used under their stated terms, and the real-camera pairs were
captured by the authors of their own environment with no identifiable
individuals. The authors declare no conflict of interest, financial or
otherwise, in the conduct or reporting of this work. The completed
Research Ethics Compliance Checklist and the Conflict-of-Interest
statement, signed and dated by both authors and the supervisor, are bound
with this appendix.

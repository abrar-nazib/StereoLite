\section{Complex Engineering Problem Attributes}
The thesis addresses a complex engineering problem, which requires depth
of knowledge beyond natural science and mathematics alone. The
load-bearing knowledge profile is K3, K4, K5, and K8. Attribute K3,
engineering fundamentals, is exercised in the stereo pipeline and the
cost-volume mechanisms of Chapters 2 and 3. Attribute K4, specialist
knowledge, is exercised in the selection and adaptation of the
tile-hypothesis, recurrent, and geometry-volume building blocks in
Chapter 3. Attribute K5, engineering design, is exercised in the design
of the StereoLite architecture and its inference-optimization pass under
hard resource constraints. Attribute K8, research-literature grounding, is
exercised in the literature review of Chapter 2, which grounds every
design choice in published evidence.

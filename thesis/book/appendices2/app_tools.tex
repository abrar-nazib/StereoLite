\chapter{List of Modern Tools Usage}
\label{app:tools}

This appendix collects the modern engineering tools, software libraries,
and hardware platforms used to design, train, optimize, deploy, and
document StereoLite. The choices favor open, widely supported tools so
that the work can be reproduced without proprietary dependencies beyond
the cloud compute itself. Table~\ref{tab:sw_tools} lists the software and
Table~\ref{tab:hw_tools} the computing and sensing hardware.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Software tools and libraries used in the thesis.}
\label{tab:sw_tools}
\small
\begin{tabular}{p{3.1cm}p{2.5cm}p{6.8cm}}
\toprule
Tool / library & Version / type & Role in the thesis\\
\midrule
Python & 3.10 & Implementation language for all the code.\\
PyTorch & 2.11 & Model definition, autograd, and training framework.\\
CUDA & 12.8 & GPU backend for training and inference.\\
Ultralytics YOLO & YOLO26s & Pretrained detection backbone for the encoder.\\
NumPy & array library & Disparity, metric, and calibration processing.\\
OpenCV & 4.x & Image I/O, stream splitting, and resizing.\\
Open3D & 0.18 & Point-cloud build, filtering, and rendering.\\
Matplotlib & plotting & Disparity, training-curve, and comparison figures.\\
Modal & cloud orchestration & Cloud A100 and T4 training and ablation jobs.\\
NVIDIA TensorRT & INT8 export & Graph optimization and eight-bit engine build.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
